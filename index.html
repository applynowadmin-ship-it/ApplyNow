Privacy and consent

This independent guide is not a university, college, NSFAS or government service. Your profile and marks are processed in this browser to calculate an estimate. Results cover only the sample courses currently included in this guide, not every South African institution or course.

When Supabase is configured and available, a package request stores your ID and learner details, marks, receipt and selected institutions in your private account so you can check the request on another device. If account setup or storage is unavailable, the app will show an error and will not confirm that your request was submitted. Package B includes your NSFAS checklist. ApplyHub SA uses the information to handle your request; details are shared with an institution only when needed to prepare an application with your permission.

Email one-time codes protect access to your account. Keep your email account secure and do not use a shared or public device. Uploading a receipt does not verify that a bank transfer happened. ApplyHub SA must check the Capitec account and approve your request before results unlock or application work starts. Applications are submitted manually; no application is filed automatically and no 24-hour turnaround is guaranteed.

For privacy questions or to ask ApplyHub SA to correct or delete your request, contact info@applyhubsa.co.za.
